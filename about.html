The Satvik Philosophy

We cook food that feeds both the body and the soul. Here is what makes our food unique:

🧅

No Onion & No Garlic

We strictly avoid onion and garlic in all our dishes. This ensures the food remains truly Satvik, promoting mental clarity, peacefulness, and high energy levels.

🔥

Freshly Prepared

No stale bases, pre-frozen rotis, or yesterday's curries. Every item—from the fluffy bhature to the stuffed parathas—is cooked fresh right upon receiving your order.

🌟

Hygienic Cooking

Our kitchen maintains strict standards of hygiene. Clean utensils, filtered water, and fresh oils are the baseline of our operations, making our street-food style highly safe.
